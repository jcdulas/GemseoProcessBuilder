# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: we want a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, accept criticism of your work gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and personal attacks are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests or anywhere the project is discussed.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/jcdulas/GemseoProcessBuilder/security/advisories/new) with "Code of conduct" in its title, or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
