# 70 — Large-scale optimizer: piloting by Claude

Status: Done, but for the acceptance by hand (a run piloted with Claude Code, left to the user)
Depends on: 69
SPEC: [LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC.md](../docs/LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC.md) § 5, 8; [CLAUDE_PILOT_SPEC.md](../docs/CLAUDE_PILOT_SPEC.md) § 4.2–4.6, 6.1

## Goal

The Claude copilot pilots `LSO_MMA` and `LSO_GCMMA` live: it sees the report of each outer iteration, and its decisions on the screening, the row cache, the asymptotes or the choice MMA ↔ GCMMA apply at the next iteration without a restart. The context sent to Claude stays small with 10⁵ to 10⁶ variables and constraints.

## Scope

In, in `gemseo-lso`:
- a listener interface on the library: the report of each outer iteration, and settings changed between two iterations (the stepper of § 5 driven from outside); the stop on request;
- the state saved on request and at the end of a run, to resume a stopped run (`resume_from` setting).

In, in `gemseo-claude-pilot`:
- telemetry: the reports in the snapshot (`algorithm_state`) and in the context, compressed;
- live settings: a new kind of application of a decision — `change_settings` on an algorithm that accepts live settings is applied at the next iteration, without ending the segment (like `change_sub_scenario`, plan 63); the guardrails check the settings against the model of the algorithm; `MMA ↔ GCMMA` is a live switch;
- detectors (§ 8): repairs at every iteration, churn of the working set, oscillating asymptotes, many GCMMA inner iterations, stale rows hurting, a row budget running out;
- large vectors summarized in the context: above a threshold, a variable or a constraint vector is sent as its size, quantiles, the count and share at bounds or active, and the largest components; the read tools give slices on request;
- the prompt: what the settings of the optimizer do and what its report means.

In the application:
- the Copilot view shows the working-set size and the rows per iteration on a chart of the History.

Out:
- piloting the row mode's parallelism (batch sizes on a cluster).

## Implementation notes

- **Order**: done before the end of plan 69, at the user's request: its conclusions wait for the results of a run piloted by Claude.
- **The live run** (`gemseo_lso/gemseo/live.py`): the library opens it on its problem for the time of a run (`live_run(problem)`), gives it each report (`publish`), and applies what was asked for at the top of each outer iteration (`apply`): settings (`change`, checked by the settings of the core: their ranges and cross checks; the settings GEMSEO owns or checks the result with, and `jacobian_mode`, are refused), a switch MMA ↔ GCMMA (`switch`; back to MMA clears the automatic switch), a stop, a save of the state. The library now drives the optimizer step by step itself.
- **Saved and resumed**: `save_state` (at the end of a run, stopped included) and `resume_from` (the run goes on at the saved iterate; a stopped run is running again, GEMSEO's criteria stop it). A run stopped at iteration 4 and resumed ends at the same iterate as the uninterrupted run, to the last bit (test, `row_refresh` `always`). The GEMSEO database of the resumed run starts again (a new execution): the starting point of the design space is evaluated again.
- **`max_row_evaluations`** (spec § 3.8, § 4, missing since plan 66): a budget of rows, status `max_rows`.
- **The pilot** imports `gemseo-lso` only for `LSO_*` algorithms, when their live run opens (at the first iteration boundary inside the run: GEMSEO evaluates the starting point before). A live switch drops the `max_iter` the guardrails add (the evaluations left): the execution running keeps its budget. A decision taken before the first report applies to the first outer iteration: the test of the switch checks that every report is GCMMA's.
- **Context**: the reports as `optimizer` (the last 10 in full, then windows of 20: mean working set, rows, repairs, inner iterations), without objective nor constraint values in `anonymized`; constraints of more than 10 components summarized at the best point (`summary_at_best`), their vectors taken before the anonymization and renamed like the constraint states. Measured: 1,050 tokens with a constraint of 10⁶ components, 2,100 with 200 reports (target 8,000).
- **Prompt**: version 2 (`prompts/system_v2.md`), the current one: the `optimizer` section, the summaries, the live application, the settings of the optimizer and what their symptoms call for (including the long tail of stress-constrained problems, plan 69).
- **Application**: the event `copilot.algorithm` needs no change in the runner nor in the UI process (forwarded like the other events, at once); `CopilotLog.algorithm` and `workingSetSeries` (pure, tested); the chart of the Copilot view is built once (a chart observes its size); the tooltip of `LineChart` names its abscissa (`xName`).
- **Acceptance by hand**: `plugins/gemseo_lso/benchmarks/run_piloted_topology.py [size] [mode]` runs the bracket of plan 69 (125 across: 10⁴ elements) as a GEMSEO scenario in row mode, `LSO_MMA` piloted by Claude Code, and writes the journal, the report and the design to `benchmarks/results/piloted/`. Checked without Claude (backend off) on a bracket of 20 across: a feasible design in 2 s. The run with Claude is left to the user.
- **After the first run piloted by Claude** (the bracket at 10⁴ elements, by the user): the run stopped after 13 outer iterations (29 of 600 evaluations) at a volume of 0.52, the plain run reaching 0.35. Two defects of the integration, not of Claude's decisions (a smaller `move_limit`, then a larger `keep_factor`, both applied live):
  - GEMSEO's `ftol_rel` compared its last evaluations, and GCMMA's inner iterations evaluate close points: taken for a stagnation. The library no longer checks GEMSEO's tolerances (`_check_stopping_criteria` keeps `max_iter` and `max_time`); `ftol_rel` and `xtol_rel` go to the core, over `stop_crit_n_x` outer iterations at feasible points. Through GEMSEO, the bracket at 1,600 elements now ends at the volume of the plain run (0.376 against 0.375).
  - The KKT residual was relative to the gradient of the objective, 1/n per variable for a volume: 10 to 10⁶ through GEMSEO, which Claude read as far from the optimum. It is relative to the largest term of the gradient of the Lagrangian (0.6 → 0.12 on a small bracket).
  - The new residual changed the trajectories and showed a gap in the restoration: near the end, a feasible point kept full moves and could leave the constraints too late. The last iterations (`restoration_iterations`, a quarter of `max_iter` at most) now shrink their moves whatever the feasibility.
  - An objective scaled by 1e4 against its constraints made MMA oscillate (the elastic cost 1000 too small against the multipliers), and one scaled by 1e-4 slowed the interior point of the dual (its tolerances are absolute). The subproblem now follows the scale of the problem: its objective and each of its constraints divided by their largest change when one variable crosses its range, the multipliers brought back to the units of the problem (LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC § 3.6). Objective and constraints scaled by 10^±4 give the iterates of the original cantilever, in as many iterations. The state gains `objective_scale` (version 6: a state saved before cannot be resumed).
  - The screening then followed the scale too (its margins were in the units of the constraints): the constraints divided by the scales of their last rows, the median for the others, the row of the largest constraint before the first screening (LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC § 3.3). Constraints of the synthetic problem multiplied by 10^±3 ask for the rows of the original. It showed that float32 rows round the dual at 1e-10 (BLAS sums in float32, steps rounded to float32): its line searches stopped 2e-5 from the solution, and a restoration ended infeasible. `row_dtype` is now `float64` by default; the bracket at 30 across converges in 62 iterations (3.1 s) instead of stopping at 80 (5.0 s).
  - Claude saw no picture of the design, only statistics of the 10⁴ densities: the analysis of the topology and a restart from another design are the object of plan 71.
