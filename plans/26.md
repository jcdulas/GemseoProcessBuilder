# 26 — Canvas ergonomics: minimap, search, auto-layout, grouping

Status: Done
Depends on: 12
SPEC: § 8.2 (minimap, search, auto-layout, group/ungroup), § 8.9

## Goal

Make large diagrams comfortable to work with: minimap, search, automatic layout with elkjs in a Web Worker, grouping, and the complete keyboard shortcut set.

## Scope

In:
- **Minimap** in the canvas corner: simplified rendering of the current level (rectangles only, reusing `lib/geometry.js`), draggable viewport rectangle, click to jump; hidden automatically on small levels.
- **Search** (Ctrl+F): overlay input searching node names and variable names (local and global) across the whole model; results list with paths; Enter/Shift+Enter to go to next/previous (navigates to the right level, centers, highlights); Esc closes.
- **Auto-layout** (Ctrl+L): elkjs `layered` algorithm with ports (`FIXED_ORDER`), direction left→right, feedback edges handled; runs in a **Web Worker** loading `vendor/elk.bundled.js`; applies to the current level or to the selection; result applied as one `moveNodes` command (undoable) with a short animation. Automatic placement of newly dropped nodes near their coupled neighbors.
- **Group / Ungroup** (Ctrl+G / Ctrl+Shift+G): commands `groupNodes` (new assembly containing the selection, positioned at their bounding box, links preserved) and `ungroupNode` (children moved to the parent at their absolute positions).
- **Duplicate** (Ctrl+D) if not already done in plan 05.
- Keyboard shortcuts of § 8.9 all implemented and listed in `Help › Keyboard shortcuts`.

Out:
- Performance work for 2,000-node models (plan 32).

## Implementation notes

- Pure libraries:
  - `lib/elk_graph.js`:
    - `toElkGraph` sends the nodes with their ports at fixed positions (`FIXED_POS`, inputs WEST, outputs EAST), and the links between laid-out nodes as port edges, or node edges when a port is hidden. ELK's layered algorithm runs left to right and breaks feedback cycles itself.
    - `positionsFromElk` keeps the top-left corner of the laid-out nodes in place.
    - `freeSpot` finds the nearest free place for a new node.
  - `lib/search_index.js`: `matchScore` ranks matches (exact, prefix, word prefix after `_ . : -`, inside); `search` puts nodes before variables and producers before consumers; `searchEntries` covers nodes, plus the global and local names of component ports taken from `resolve.couplings`. The ports of nested drivers are left out: their components are shown instead.
- ELK in a Web Worker. Chromium refuses worker scripts of the `gpb://` scheme ("cannot be accessed from origin 'null'"), but a worker started from a Blob may `importScripts` from it.
  - The Blob sets `self.GPB_BASE` and imports `js/workers/elk_worker.js`, which imports elkjs's official worker script `vendor/elk-worker.min.js`, newly vendored: elkjs 0.12.0, EPL-2.0, listed in `vendor/README.md`.
  - The page uses `new ELK({workerFactory})`. `elk.bundled.js` cannot serve as a worker by itself: its dispatcher only starts with the in-page FakeWorker.
  - If the worker fails, ELK runs on the page (`window.ELK`). On the SSBJ System level, the worst frame gap during the layout is 18 ms.
- Auto-layout (Ctrl+L, `views/canvas/auto_layout.js`): the selected nodes (2 or more) or the whole level, animated for 250 ms and stored as one `moveNodes` (undoable), then the view fits once redrawn. "Add at center" (double-click in the Library) now picks a free place near the middle of the view with `freeSpot`. Placing a node near its coupled neighbours would need its couplings before it is added, so it is left out.
- Minimap (`views/canvas/minimap.js`): the nodes of the level as rectangles and the visible area; click or drag to move the view. Hidden below 6 nodes or while the whole level is visible.
- Search (Ctrl+F, `views/canvas/search.js`): an overlay with a result count and a list. Enter / Shift+Enter (or the arrows) go to the next or previous result: open its level, select and center the node, and flash it. Escape closes.
- Commands (`core/commands.py`):
  - `groupNodes`: siblings go into a new assembly at their bounding box, with positions relative to it, at the place of the first node; links are unchanged.
  - `ungroupNode`: the children return at absolute positions and the assembly is deleted.
  - Each is the other's exact inverse: placements, index, assembly fields, and the `placed` flag for assemblies without a position. They are in the generic undo/redo round trip test and in `test_commands_grouping.py`.
  - Actions `model.group` and `model.ungroup` select the result.
- Every § 8.9 shortcut is an action definition, so Help › Keyboard shortcuts lists them all.
- Smoke-tested:
  - SSBJ System level: a readable left-to-right layout, computed in the worker, and undo restores it.
  - Searching `y_1` opens `System › StructureOptimizer` and selects Structure.
  - On the 500-component model: the minimap appears when zoomed in; group, then ungroup, then two undos give 48 → 50 → 48 → 50 nodes.
