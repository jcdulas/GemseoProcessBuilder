# 55 — Wing sizing in bi-level on surrogates

Status: Done
Depends on: 54
SPEC: § 15.3

## Goal

A demo taking the wing sizing again in bi-level (the user's request):
- written in several files that follow the graph of the study;
- for the aerodynamics and the structure, a normal model and a surrogate trained on it (a duo);
- the optimization loops on the surrogates, and its optimum is checked on the normal models.

## Scope

In `examples/wingBiLevel/`:
- `wing_bilevel.py`: SystemOptimizer (area and span, maximizing the range with the wing loading at most 3000), and the loop: optimize on the surrogates, check on the normal models, and while the range differs by more than 1 %, let the surrogates learn the normal models around the optimum and optimize again (4 rounds at most);
- `aero_optimizer/`: AeroOptimizer (the camber, minimizing the drag), its normal model (`aero_model.py`) and its duo;
- `structure_optimizer/`: StructureOptimizer (the thickness of the wing box, minimizing the weight), its normal model (`structure_model.py`) and its duo;
- `performance.py`: the range (Breguet) and the wing loading;
- `duo.py`: `ModelDuo`, a normal model and its surrogate: samples, training, loading, enriching;
- `validation.py`: the optimum evaluated by an MDA of the surrogates and by an MDA of the normal models.

Out:
- Changes to the application: it reads the study as it is.

## Implementation notes

- **Trained without executing disciplines**: the surrogates learn the physics functions of the normal models, not the disciplines. The application reads a study by running it until its first execution: a discipline executed while training would be taken for the study.
- **Ranges widened by 2 %** for the samples, so that the bounds of the local variables, where the sub-optimizations often end, are inside the domain the surrogates learnt (GEMSEO warned at each evaluation otherwise).
- **Enriching** needs the couplings at the optimum (the weight for the aerodynamics, the load for the structure): they come from the evaluation on the normal models.
- The local variables at the optimum of the system come from its database, where BiLevel stores the results of the sub-optimizations.
- The loop is `optimize_and_validate`, not `main`: the application rewrites `main` when it saves a script, and keeps the functions of the user.
- No test reads this demo: training its surrogates takes longer than one second. The Sobieski demo in several files covers the same reading.
- The trained surrogates (`examples/wingBiLevel/models/`) are not versioned.
