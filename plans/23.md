# 23 — Nested drivers, BiLevel and local parallelism

Status: Done
Depends on: 17
SPEC: § 6.1 (transparent), § 6.2 (BiLevel), § 6.3, § 6.4 (Interface, Execution), § 11.3 (n_processes)

## Goal

Support drivers inside other containers (a DOE around an optimization, BiLevel sub-scenarios), the driver Interface tab, and local parallel evaluation of DOEs.

## Scope

In:
- Resolver: a nested driver opens its own scope; seen from its parent it exposes only its **exposed inputs** and **exposed outputs** (hook left in plan 11).
- Driver editor **Interface** tab: pick exposed inputs (among the scope's free inputs that the parent should provide) and exposed outputs (among objectives, constraints, observables and other outputs); advanced adapter settings (`reset_x0_before_opt`, `set_x0_before_opt`, …) through a generated form when GEMSEO exposes one, otherwise explicit fields.
- Codegen:
  - nested driver → `MDOScenarioAdapter(sub_scenario, input_names, output_names, …)` built in its own function (`build_<name>_adapter()`), with a comment explaining the adapter;
  - BiLevel: the system driver's child optimization drivers are passed as sub-scenarios to the `BiLevel` formulation, with its settings;
  - assemblies with `transparent` flattened (already in plan 14) — check the interplay with nested drivers.
- Validation rules: nested driver without exposed outputs, exposed input not free, BiLevel without sub-scenarios, BiLevel sub-scenario design variables overlapping the system design variables (warning).
- Runner: status events for disciplines inside nested scenarios (mapping sidecar includes nested ids); progress reported for the outer driver, with inner progress as a secondary indicator.
- Local parallelism: `n_processes` in the Execution tab for DOE drivers; codegen passes it; runner reports aggregated progress only (no per-discipline status when `n_processes > 1`) and the UI shows why.
- Examples: `examples/sobieski_bilevel.gpb.json`, `examples/doe_around_optimization.gpb.json`, with golden scripts.

Out:
- XDSM of nested processes (plan 25 covers XDSM in general).

## Design

- One-second rule: SSBJ BiLevel with full settings is far too slow for a test. Tests use reduced settings (1–2 system iterations, 2–3 sub-iterations, small DOE); if even that exceeds one second, test the generation (golden + dry run) and test sub-scenarios separately; record the choice here and in SPEC § 17 risk 9.

## Implementation notes

- Resolver (`core/resolver.py`):
  - A driver opens its own scope. Seen from its parent, it is a discipline whose ports are:
    - for an MDA driver, the derived ports of its content;
    - for a sub-scenario of a BiLevel optimization, its free inputs minus its design variables in, and its outputs plus its design variables out (what BiLevel exchanges);
    - otherwise, the lists of `config["interface"]`.
  - These virtual ports are coupled in the parent scope, deepest drivers first. `Resolution.representatives` and `component_port()` lead from a virtual port to a component port inside (an input for a design variable). `driver_variables` and the structure rules (types, free inputs, unused outputs) follow them.
- Model (`core/drivers.py`):
  - `Interface`: `inputs`, `outputs`, `reset_x0_before_opt`, `set_x0_before_opt` (exclusive with the reset), `set_bounds_before_opt`, `keep_opt_history`, in `DriverConfig.interface`.
  - Helpers `is_nested` (a driver directly in the model is a study of its own) and `is_bilevel_sub_scenario`.
- Rules (`core/rules/nested.py`):
  - errors: `nested_without_outputs`, `exposed_input_not_free`, `exposed_output_unknown`, `bilevel_without_sub_scenarios`;
  - warning: `bilevel_shared_design_variable`.
- Codegen (`codegen/nested.py`):
  - `build_<name>_adapter()` returns `MDOScenarioAdapter(build_<name>_scenario(), inputs, outputs, …)`, with a comment explaining the adapter. It is written before the scenario it wraps, so the script reads top-down.
  - `build_<name>_scenario()` creates the children, an inline design space and the scenario, then calls `set_algorithm(...)` (a nested parametric study gets `build_<name>_samples()`).
  - BiLevel sub-scenarios are passed as they are to `build_disciplines()`, typed `list[Discipline | MDOScenario]`; `FORMULATION_COMMENTS` explains BiLevel.
  - A nested MDA driver becomes an `MDAChain` function.
  - `scenarios.py` was refactored (`scenario_lines`, `algorithm_arguments`) to share the code with top-level scenarios.
  - The mapping gains `scenarios` (driver id → scenario name), `functions` (function → driver, so dry-run errors inside a nested scenario point at its driver), the adapters as `<Name>_adapter` in `disciplines`, and `n_processes`.
- Runner:
  - `walk` follows `adapter.scenario`, and `top_disciplines` adds the adapters BiLevel creates, so the disciplines inside nested scenarios and the nested drivers themselves report their states.
  - `InnerProgressListener` sends `inner_progress` events: iterations of the current run, counted with `len(database)`, since the adapter resets the database for each run.
  - BiLevel deep-copies the sub-scenario databases (`keep_opt_history`), so the observers return themselves from `__deepcopy__`.
  - With `n_processes > 1`, no discipline observers; `progress` carries `processes`.
- Page:
  - Interface tab (`panels/driver_editor/interface_tab.js`) for drivers inside another node: inputs and outputs picked among the free inputs, and among the outputs plus the design variables (`interfaceCandidates`), with the advanced adapter settings.
  - Under BiLevel, the tab only explains what BiLevel exchanges. `placement()` and `tabsFor(kind, placement)` are pure and tested.
  - Execution tab: "Processes" only for DOE and parametric drivers, with the reason the component states are missing.
  - Status bar: secondary "<Scenario>: iteration n" and "N processes" indicators. The project path now shrinks (`min-width: 0`), so the run item stays visible.
  - A maximized objective (`-y_4`) is recognized as the objective in the live Results views.
- Examples and tests:
  - `examples/sobieski_bilevel.gpb.json`: GEMSEO's SSBJ BiLevel with COBYQA (NLopt is not installed) at 20 iterations and SLSQP sub-optimizations at 30.
  - `examples/doe_around_optimization.gpb.json`: an LHS on `alpha` around the Sellar MDF optimization (pyDOE is not installed). Both have golden scripts.
  - Equivalence with hand-written GEMSEO uses reduced settings: BiLevel with 2 system and 3 sub-iterations in about 0.5 s; DOE with 2 samples and 5 iterations in about 0.2 s. SPEC § 17 risk 9 is settled.
- Smoke-tested:
  - The SSBJ BiLevel run from the UI gives y_4 = 1948.2166 with the same x_shared as the hand-written GEMSEO script, to all printed digits.
  - The status bar shows the sub-optimization iterations.
  - The DOE around the optimization runs 5 samples, and the nested Optimizer shows its interface ports and state on the Study canvas.
