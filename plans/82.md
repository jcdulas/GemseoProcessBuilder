## Goal

Plan 76 chains the rows of the constraints through an intermediate discipline offering `AdjointJacobian`, its Jacobian never formed, but left the gradient of the objective to GEMSEO: an objective read from the outputs of such an intermediate (a compliance, a work of the loads) made GEMSEO linearize the intermediate in full at every point, the cost the protocol avoids. Its notes leave "chaining the objective's gradient through the products as well (one more row of weights)" to a later plan; the owner asked for it on 2026-10-23.

## Scope

In:
- `objective_source(problem, variables)` in `gemseo_lso.gemseo.sources`: the objective chained when a discipline upstream of it offers `AdjointJacobian`; its sign -1 when GEMSEO maximizes it (GEMSEO minimizes its opposite);
- `Chaining`: the objective as one more row of the reverse accumulation (the same pass as the rows, its upstream disciplines in the union of the chains), its own discipline linearized for the objective alone, once per point;
- `GemseoProblem.objective_gradient`: the chained gradient when there is one, GEMSEO's otherwise;
- tests against GEMSEO's gradient on the path without the products (normalized or not, minimized or maximized), a whole run, an objective that does not read the loads.

Out:
- the gradients of the equality constraints (still GEMSEO's);
- the protocols of the objective's own discipline (below, implementation notes);
- coupled disciplines (open point 5): an objective in an MDA keeps GEMSEO's gradient.

## Implementation notes

- **When the objective is chained** (choice made at implementation, accepted by the owner's standing rule of 2026-10-19): only when a discipline upstream of the objective offers `AdjointJacobian`, the case where GEMSEO's gradient would form the Jacobian of the intermediate. Otherwise, and for an objective computed from several outputs, or by a discipline in an MDA, or upstream of a coupling, the gradient stays GEMSEO's: the behaviour of every existing problem is unchanged.
- **The objective's own discipline is linearized** for the objective alone (`add_differentiated_outputs([objective])`, once per point, a copy kept for the point), even when it offers `RowJacobian` or `AdjointJacobian`: a discipline giving the rows of its constraints is not required to give those of another output (`RowsLocal` of the tests gives its constraint rows for any output name), so its protocols are asked for the constraints only. The objective is a scalar: its Jacobian is one row per input, cheap beside that of the intermediate.
- **Sign**: -1 when `problem.minimize_objective` is False (GEMSEO's objective is then the opposite of the output); the design space normalization by the ranges, as for the rows.
- The objective's row goes through `Chaining.rows` in a call of its own (the core asks for the gradient and the rows apart): one product call per point for the objective, one per batch of rows for the constraints, as measured by the tests (`loads.product_rows == [1]`).
- The GEMSEO database no longer receives the objective's gradient when it is chained (as it receives no row of a constraint given row by row).
