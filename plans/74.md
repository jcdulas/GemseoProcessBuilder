## Implementation notes

- Defects fixed: L-BFGS-B ending on a failed line search without converging (restarts); the interior point not converging silently (`converged` flag, `checked` falls back to the dual solver); an inaccurate reduced system (`solve_scaled`); the automatic dual solver for few constraints and many variables (L-BFGS-B when at most 10 constraints and 500 variables; the interior point only when `variables × constraints²` is at most 10⁷).
- Not fixed, recorded in spec § 9: sensitivity of MMA to the width of the bounds; GCMMA landing in the local optimum of the 10-bar truss; slow interior point on hard small subproblems; `newton` chosen explicitly on a dense single-row problem.
- Added on request: the path through the constraints, `descent_iterations` (off by default; spec § 3.8), a descent with soft constraints before the restoration. On the 10-bar truss, GCMMA with it reaches 5060.85 lb where it lands in 5076.85 lb without it (71 evaluations against 41); MMA with 10 iterations of descent does the opposite, so it is not a general cure.
- Added on request: the pilot tests for a local optimum. The reports carry `at_bound` and `bound_costs` (the exit cost of the cheapest variables at a bound), a `frozen` event fires when the KKT residual falls below 100 times its tolerance with variables at a bound, and prompt v6 asks for one test by `steer` (lifting the cheapest variables to a third of their range). On the 10-bar truss with 15 s per iteration, one run escaped the local optimum (5060.88 lb, three components lifted by Claude); another did not (its bar 6 was 0.007 from its bound, not at it, so it was not listed, and Claude lifted two other bars).
- The 72-bar truss: stories of 60 in, numbered from the top.
