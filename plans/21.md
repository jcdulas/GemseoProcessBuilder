## Goal

Complete the interactive results views with multi-variable exploration, linked brushing, parametric study views and run comparison, while staying in SVG for large datasets.

## Scope

In:
- **Scatter matrix**: variable selection, coloring by feasibility or by a variable, brushing in any cell.
- **XY plot**: any two variables, optional color variable, optional connecting line in iteration order.
- **Parallel coordinates**: axis reordering, per-axis brush filters.
- **Linked brushing**: a shared selection (set of row indices) across scatter matrix, XY, parallel coordinates and Data table (table shows "selected only" toggle).
- **Parametric**: one variable → line chart per response; two variables → heat map with contour lines (d3-contour is part of d3 v7).
- **Compare runs**: select several runs of the same driver (or compatible variables) → overlay histories and summaries side by side.
- **Large volumes** (≥ 10,000 points): display-time decimation and hex/rectangular binning in scatter cells, computed in the worker (`results.binned(run, x, y, bins)`) or in pure JS for small data.

Out:
- Native GEMSEO post-processings (plan 22).

## Implementation notes

- Worker (`results/reader.py`):
  - `matrix(names, max_rows=5000)` returns some columns of every evaluation, or of 5,000 evenly spaced ones beyond that.
  - `binned(x, y, bins, filters)` counts points in bins over every evaluation. The pure `bin_counts` uses the same formula as `lib/binning.js`: range min–max, a constant widened by ±0.5, `floor((v - low) / (high - low) * bins)` clipped, the highest value in the last bin, NaN left out.
  - `rows` and `export_csv` accept `evaluations` (a brushed selection).
  - Bridge: `results.matrix`, `results.binned`.
- Pure JS libraries:
  - `lib/binning.js`; a shared fixture `tests/js/fixtures/binning_case.json` is checked by both the JS test and `tests/python/results/test_binning.py`.
  - `lib/decimate.js`: extremes-keeping decimation, moved out of the accumulator, plus `evenPositions`.
  - `lib/grid_from_samples.js`: levels, a full-factorial grid with missing points as `null`, and bilinear `resample` for smooth contours.
- `services/brush_selection.js` holds the brushed evaluation numbers per run and the view they came from; "Clear selection" clears it everywhere.
- Views (`views/results/`, sharing `common.js`: variable choices, colors by feasibility or by a variable, selection helpers):
  - `scatter_matrix.js`:
    - 2 to 6 chosen variables, histograms on the diagonal, color by feasibility, by a variable or none;
    - a brush in any cell (one at a time); unselected points are greyed out;
    - from `BINNING_THRESHOLD = 10,000` evaluations, cells show worker-computed bins shaded on a log scale, without brushing (documented in the view).
  - `xy_plot.js`: any X and Y, color, optional line in evaluation order, tooltips, brush.
  - `parallel_coordinates.js`: up to 10 axes, reordered by dragging their titles, a brush per axis (the selection is the intersection), 2,000 lines drawn at most.
  - `parametric.js`: CustomDOE runs only. One variable gives one curve per response; two give a heat map of the chosen response over the level grid, with smooth contour lines (bilinear resampling ×12), a color legend and tooltips.
  - `compare.js`: summaries side by side (status, evaluations, algorithm, formulation, best objective, feasibility, x_opt), and objective histories overlaid when the runs share their objective.
- Data table: a "Selected only" toggle; brushed rows are highlighted, and the CSV export keeps the selection.
- Runs panel: a "Compare" checkbox column and a "Compare the N ticked runs" button.
- Smoke-tested:
  - On the Rosenbrock DOE (30 samples), a selection made in the scatter matrix shows the same 5 points in parallel coordinates (5 of 30 lines), the Table (5 rows with "Selected only") and the XY plot (25 dimmed).
  - The Rosenbrock parametric study gives a 5×3 heat map with 9 smooth contour paths, and 2 runs are compared (table and 2 curves).
  - On a 20,000-sample LHS, the scatter matrix shows 2,196 bins and parallel coordinates draw 2,000 lines in about 3 s. It stays usable; the benchmark belongs to plan 32.
