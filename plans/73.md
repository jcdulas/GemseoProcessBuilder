# 73 — Large-scale optimizer: the KKT residual at the end of a run, and the stop criteria

Status: To do
Depends on: 70
SPEC: [LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC.md](../docs/LARGE_SCALE_OPTIMIZER_SPEC.md) § 3.3, 3.7, 3.8, 4, 6, 9

## Goal

A run stops for a reason that does not depend on noise, and says truthfully where it stands. Today, at the end of the runs of the stress-constrained bracket, the KKT residual oscillates between 10⁻³ and 5·10⁻² instead of decreasing: whether a run stops `converged`, `stalled` or at `max_iter` then depends on its path. Five runs of the bracket at 18 across, the constraints multiplied by 1, 1000 and 1 ± 10⁻¹⁵, stopped `converged` at 59 and 63 iterations or ran to `max_iter` (80) at the same volume within 0.4 % (plan 70, notes): the design had stopped improving long before, the criteria did not see it.

Independent of plans 71 and 72: it can be done before them.

## What is known

- The residual is measured with the multipliers of the last subproblem (§ 3.8), solved to an adaptive tolerance (a tenth of the last step, § 3.7), at the previous iterate, on the working set of that subproblem: an inexact subproblem, a working set that changes, or a constraint entering or leaving it, all change the multipliers, hence the residual, without the design changing.
- The stress constraints of a topology problem are degenerate near the optimum: many near-active constraints with correlated rows, their multipliers not unique; the subproblem picks some, the next picks others.
- A variable within 10⁻⁸ of its range from a bound is at the bound; a density at 10⁻⁶ is not, and its gradient counts in the residual although the optimizer will never move it farther (plan 70: the interior point left variables 2.5·10⁻⁶ from their bound).
- MMA switches to GCMMA when its KKT residual cycles (§ 3.2); GCMMA's inner iterations also change the step, hence the multipliers.
- `stalled` needs the iterates to move less than 10⁻³ of the ranges; `ftol_rel` and `xtol_rel` are 0 in the core by default (GEMSEO passes its own).

## Scope

1. **Diagnose first**, on the bracket (18 and 30 across) and the synthetic problem, with the reports extended as needed (kept if useful to the pilot):
   - the residual split into stationarity and complementarity, and the constraints and variables it comes from at each iteration;
   - the multipliers of the subproblem against multipliers **estimated at the iterate** (the least-squares nonnegative multipliers of the near-active constraints of the working set, from the rows at the iterate: a bounded NNLS on a few hundred columns);
   - the effect of the dual tolerance, of the working set changes, of the bound tolerance and of GCMMA's inner iterations on the residual.
   The findings go into the implementation notes before any fix.
2. **Fix the measure** as the diagnosis says; the candidates:
   - the residual with multipliers estimated at the iterate (the ones above), which do not depend on the subproblem's path; the subproblem's multipliers kept for screening and warm starts;
   - a bound activity that follows the optimizer: a variable at its bound when its move limit or asymptote keeps it there, not only within 10⁻⁸;
   - the residual of degenerate constraints measured on their cone (a set of multipliers exists), not on the one the subproblem picked.
3. **Revise the stop criteria** (§ 3.8), so that the status tells what happened:
   - `converged`: the KKT residual below `kkt_tolerance`, as now, with the new measure;
   - `stalled`: at a feasible point, the best objective has not improved by more than a relative `ftol_rel` over a window (default on in the core, not only through GEMSEO), whatever the residual — the design no longer improves; the residual reached stated in the message;
   - `xtol`: the iterates no longer move;
   - `max_iter`: only when the design was still improving;
   - the default values chosen on the benchmarks, the core and the GEMSEO plugin consistent (GEMSEO's `ftol_rel`, `xtol_rel`, `stop_crit_n_x` mapped as since plan 70).
4. **The pilot** (plan 70): the reports give the new measure and why the run stopped; the detectors that read the residual (and `stuck`, plan 71, if done) use it.

Out:

- global optimization: runs ending in different local optima (the bracket's 0.4 % between runs) are for the restarts of plan 71;
- a new solver of the subproblem.

## Tests

- The KKT residual at a known optimum of a degenerate problem (two identical constraints: multipliers not unique) is zero whatever the multipliers of the subproblem.
- A variable held at its bound by its move limits is at the bound for the residual.
- On the bracket at 18 across (under one second: a few iterations from a saved state near the end), the residual decreases, or the run stops `stalled` with the residual reached in its message.
- **Robustness**: the bracket with its constraints multiplied by 1, 1000 and 1 ± 10⁻¹⁵ stops with the same status, and at a number of iterations within 10 % (a benchmark, run by hand; a small version as a test if it fits in one second).
- The existing problems (cantilever, HS, synthetic, Sellar, Rosenbrock) still converge, with the same statuses.
- Every test under one second.
