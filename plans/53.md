# 53 — A study in several files

Status: Done
Depends on: 52
SPEC: § 4.2.1

## Goal

The bi-level example becomes a study written by hand as a GEMSEO user would: several `.py` files, functions and classes, and a component of every kind the application offers (the user's request). Reading it showed what the reader still missed.

## Scope

In:
- `examples/demoBiLevel/`: the study (`demo_bilevel.py`), its sub-optimizations (`local_problems.py`, a dataclass), its economics (`economics.py`: analytic, Python function behind a `RemappingDiscipline`, a `Discipline` class, a chain and a parallel chain), a surrogate trained and pickled on the first run (`maintenance.py`), an external code (`noise/`);
- the reader: `set_algorithm` of nested scenarios, surrogates pickled by the user, modules of the study read again;
- the introspection of a surrogate without metadata.

Out:
- Reading scripts whose disciplines come from other GEMSEO wrappers than the application's executable one (`DiscFromExe`): they stay Python class components.

## Implementation notes

- **Nested algorithms**: without `set_algorithm` recorded, the sub-optimizations lost their settings (`max_iter=30`), and the generated script diverged from the original after the first iterations.
- **Modules forgotten**: the worker kept the modules of a study imported at the first reading; an edited module was not seen when the study was opened again. They are compared as strings (not resolved paths): resolving the thousands of loaded modules made each reading last up to a second.
- **Surrogates pickled by the user** have no metadata: the summary gives the type of the model and its file, and the ports are read by loading the model.
- **Python functions** annotated with `NDArray[float64]`, with defaults held in module constants: annotated with `float`, `AutoPyDiscipline` of GEMSEO 6 refuses the arrays other disciplines give.
- **mypy strict** refuses subclassing `Discipline`, typed `Any` since GEMSEO has no annotations: the example says so on the line (`type: ignore[misc]`).
- `add_observable` with a list makes one observable of all the names: the example adds them one by one.
- The trained model (`examples/demoBiLevel/models/`) is not versioned: it is trained on the first run.
