Status: Done
Depends on: 53

## Goal

The Library lists the example studies as demos, besides the components, containers and drivers (the user's request): a click opens one, in the installed application too.

## Scope

In:
- `core/demos.py`: the demos of a folder, their titles and summaries, and their copies;
- `app/api_demos.py`: `demos.list` and `demos.open`;
- the Demos section of the Library;
- the examples shipped in the package as `gemseo_process_builder/demos`;
- the smoke tool checking that the installed package has them.

Out:
- Updating a copy made before when the demo changes in a new version.

## Implementation notes

- **Which scripts are demos**: those whose docstring says `Run it with: python <their name>` — the generated examples, and the bi-level study written by hand, whose title was shortened to fit the Library. No list to keep up to date.
- **Copies**: in the Documents of the user, never in the installation or the repository, since a project writes next to its script. A copy made before is opened again rather than replaced, keeping the changes of the user.
- **Packaging**: `force-include` put in the wheel what local runs leave in `examples/` (runs, autosaves, a trained model); `only-include` with `sources` keeps the exclusions and `.gitignore`. Every file of the package is still in the wheel.
- The Library opens a demo with a single click (a demo is not dragged onto the canvas); its tile has the accent color and the open icon.
