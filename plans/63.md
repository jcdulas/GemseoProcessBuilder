## Goal

The copilot pilots the other studies the spec names: a DOE, in sampling segments with *Add samples* where the responses need more; the system optimization of a BiLevel study, whose sub-optimizations it may retune between two system iterations; and it tells how far the surrogate components of a study can be trusted.

## Scope

In, in the plugin:
- DOE: a DOE scenario is run in sampling segments; the budget is its `n_samples`; *Add samples* (optionally in a sub-region, whose bounds hold for that segment only) starts a new segment with a new seed; *Stop*; no warm start, no detector events;
- BiLevel: the sub-scenarios of a BiLevel formulation in the snapshot and the context; a new action, `change_sub_scenario` (another algorithm and/or settings of a sub-optimization), checked against the settings model of its algorithm and applied at the next system iteration without ending the segment; anonymized sub-scenario names;
- surrogates: a surrogate component described by its model, its training size and its R² on its training data;
- the prompt: DOE, BiLevel and surrogates.

In, in the application:
- the Copilot tab and codegen for DOE drivers; the actions offered by driver kind (DOE: *Add samples*, *Stop*; a BiLevel system: *Change the sub-optimizations* too);
- the validation rule: DOE drivers run on their own may be piloted; a parametric study may not.

Out:
- Piloting the sub-optimizations of a BiLevel study with their own segments.

## Implementation notes

- **Open point 2 prototyped on a small analytic BiLevel study** (two sub-optimizations of a local variable under a system optimization of a shared one, `tests/pilot_samples.py`), not on the wing sizing demo, to keep the tests under one second. GEMSEO's adapter runs `scenario.execute()` with the settings stored by `set_algorithm`, so the pilot calls `set_algorithm` on the sub-scenario at the next system iteration; the adapters are not rebuilt. The same algorithm keeps its other settings, another one starts from its defaults. The wing sizing demo remains to be tried by hand, with the piloted DOE.
- **A DOE region never changes the bounds.** GEMSEO sets the design space to the best point of the whole database at the end of each DOE (`_post_run`), which raises when the bounds were narrowed and the best point lies outside them. The points of a region are drawn with `compute_doe` in a copy of the design space narrowed to it, then evaluated with `CustomDOE`; the segment keeps the method and the number of samples Claude chose.
- **Half first.** When *Add samples* is allowed and the method has a seed, the first segment draws half of `n_samples`; when Claude asks for no more, the samples left are drawn in one last segment with the user's method and settings (reason: "the samples left, drawn as the user asked"). A method without a seed (`DiagonalDOE`, `OATDOE`, full factorial…) would draw the same points again: it keeps all its samples. A later segment of a seeded method gets another seed (`index + 1`), unless the user or Claude set one.
- **A DOE without `n_samples`** (`CustomDOE`) runs as set, without the copilot (`disabled`: "a DOE needs n_samples to be piloted"); the validation rule warns about it.
- **Components of a BiLevel study**: `components_of` goes down into the sub-scenarios (a scenario has no `io`), so Claude sees the disciplines of all levels; the system reports no gradients.
- **Actions by driver kind**: `CopilotAction` gains `change_sub_scenario` and `add_samples`; `copilot_actions(kind, formulation)` (Python) and `actionsFor(kind, formulation)` (JS) give those that apply. The Copilot tab lists only those; codegen writes `allowed_actions` only when the user unticked one of them. A project saved before this plan, with the four optimization actions stored, gets `allowed_actions` written for a BiLevel system until *Retune a sub-optimization* is ticked.
