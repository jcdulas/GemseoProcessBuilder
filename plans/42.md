# 42 — Optimization guidance and algorithm guides

Status: Done
Depends on: 41
SPEC: § 6.4

## Goal

The user asked for help to create an optimization, and for information on the algorithms, "so that we have an idea of what they do". GEMSEO describes them in a few words ("Dual annealing", "Halton sequence").

## Scope

In:
- `lib/algorithm_guide.js`:
  - a guide of each optimization algorithm, sampling method and formulation of GEMSEO (family, what it does, when, cost);
  - the suggested algorithm and its reasons;
  - the order by family;
- `panels/driver_editor/algorithm_guide.js`: the guide card, the suggestion, the comparison dialog;
- in the Algorithm and Formulation tabs: the card, the suggestion, *Compare…*, the family in the options;
- tips in the Design variables, Objectives and Constraints tabs;
- optional Constraints and Formulation steps in the optimization checklist.

Out:
- Guides of the settings of each algorithm (the forms show GEMSEO's descriptions).

## Implementation notes

- **Guides**:
  - Written by hand, from what each method is and from GEMSEO's capabilities (checked on GEMSEO 6.3: gradients, inequality and equality constraints, integers, several objectives).
  - An algorithm added by a GEMSEO plugin has no guide: the card shows GEMSEO's description, and the comparison puts it under "Other".
- **Suggestion**:
  - It uses the worst origin of the derivatives of the components under the driver (§ 9.3); while the origins are unknown, it assumes derivatives.
  - It refreshes when the origins arrive.
  - It is never an algorithm that cannot solve the problem.
- **Test tool**: the scripted runs of the app must print in UTF-8 (`PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`). The "✓" of the suggestion made the print fail on the Windows console encoding, which looked like a frozen page.
