# 25 — XDSM view

Status: Not started
Depends on: 16
SPEC: § 8.5, § 13 (XDSM export), § 17 (risk 5)

## Goal

Show the XDSM of a driver's process, produced by GEMSEO from the generated scenario and rendered in SVG with d3, with HTML and PDF exports.

## Scope

In:
- Worker method `xdsm.build(source)`: imports the generated script, calls `build_scenario()`, runs GEMSEO's XDSM generation to obtain the XDSM JSON (without opening a browser), returns it.
- Renderer decision: evaluate vendoring **xdsmjs** (license, d3 v7 compatibility, offline use, ability to theme with our tokens) against a custom d3 renderer of the XDSM JSON. Choose, record the decision in the notes and in SPEC § 17 risk 5.
- XDSM center tab: renders the XDSM of the selected driver (or the top driver), with zoom/pan, and the process animation steps if the chosen renderer supports them; refresh when the model changes (debounced, only when the tab is visible).
- Click on an XDSM discipline box selects the matching node (via the mapping sidecar).
- Exports: standalone HTML (renderer + JSON inlined) and PDF via pyxdsm when installed and LaTeX is available (menu item disabled with a tooltip otherwise).

Out:
- PNG/SVG image export shared by all views (plan 31).
