## Goal

In row mode, the rows of a constraint whose inputs come from an intermediate discipline are chained by vector-Jacobian products, and the full Jacobian of that intermediate is never formed. In the inner sizing of a structure, the reserve factors are asked for row by row through the internal loads (`nastran_sv`'s `DesignProblem.vjp`, one adjoint per row and subcase), instead of the Jacobian of the loads, which costs one solve per design variable. Milestone M3 of the sizing loop (ECOSYSTEM-EVOLUTIONS.md § 6).
