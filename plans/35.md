# 35 — Drivers as tiles of the workflow

Status: Done
Depends on: 34
SPEC: § 6.2, § 8.2

## Goal

Show drivers as tiles of the workflow, next to the nodes they drive, instead of containers drawn around them. The user asked: "the optimizer should be a tile of the workflow, not above it and enclosing it; it will be simpler to understand". Choices made with the user:

- the links of a driver are a loop of variables: the design variables go from the driver to the nodes that use them, and the objectives, constraints and responses come back;
- a node is put under the control of a driver by linking the driver to it;
- every driver kind is a tile (optimization, DOE, parametric study, MDA); assemblies stay groups (cards or frames).

The data model does not change: a driver still contains the nodes it drives, as in GEMSEO.

## Scope

In:
- `lib/driver_links.js` (pure):
  - the variables a driver sends and receives, by kind;
  - its links to the nodes it drives, from the view of its scope;
  - a control link to a driven node that exchanges no variable with it.
- `lib/scene.js`:
  - a driver is a tile, and the nodes it drives are drawn at the same level, in the same coordinates;
  - the couplings of its scope are resolved and drawn with the level;
  - a tile overlapping the nodes it drives is shown on their left.
- Drawing:
  - the tile shows the kind and the number of nodes it drives;
  - driver links are in the color of the driver, with a count badge;
  - control links are thin and dashed.
- Interactions:
  - dragging from a tile onto a node (or from a node onto a tile) moves the node under that driver;
  - the panel of a driver link lists its variables and their roles, with **Stop driving** (the node goes back to the level of the driver);
  - double-clicking a tile opens the driver editor;
  - "Expand in place" is for assemblies only.
- Auto-layout of a level with tiles, examples laid out again, SPEC § 8.2 and the user guide.

Out:
- Changes to the data model, the resolver or the generated scripts.

## Implementation notes

- **Model unchanged**: a driver still contains the nodes it drives (as in GEMSEO); only the canvas changes. The resolver, the validation and the generated scripts are untouched.
- **Coordinates**: the nodes of a driver are stored in the coordinates of the level where the driver tile is shown (the same frame as the tile). So:
  - moving a tile does not move the other nodes;
  - putting a node under a driver does not move it on the canvas;
  - opening the driver's level alone (context menu › Open alone) shows the nodes where they were.
  - Projects laid out before tiles had the children in the driver's own level. When a tile overlaps the nodes it drives, the scene shows it on their left (`TILE_GAP`), and dragging it stores the new position.
- **`lib/driver_links.js`** (pure, tested):
  - `driverVariables(kind, config)`: the variables a driver sends and receives. Optimization: design variables out; objectives, constraints and observables back. DOE: design variables out; responses and observables back. Parametric: the variables of the levels out, responses back. MDA: none.
  - `driverLinks` matches them with the global names of the ports of the driven nodes, from the resolved view of the driver's scope. A node exchanging nothing with its driver gets a control link; so does every node until the scope is resolved.
- **`lib/scene.js`**:
  - `isTile(node)` is true for every driver;
  - `levelsToResolve` always includes the drivers;
  - the nodes of a tile are placed at the depth of the tile (selectable, draggable, in auto-layout and in the minimap like the others) with `parent` the driver;
  - driver links have the kinds `driver` and `control`, with `driver` and `tone` fields; a result going back to the driver is routed under the nodes, like a feedback.
- **Gestures and panels**:
  - `views/canvas/drive.js`: `driveNode` (`reparentNodes` into the driver, with a toast), `stopDriving` (back to the driver's parent), and the panel of a driver link.
  - Link drawing: a card-to-node gesture involving a driver drives the node instead of opening the variable panel.
  - Double-clicking a tile opens the driver editor. The context menu of a tile has Open alone, without Expand in place or Variables.
  - `edit.selectAll` and the auto-layout state count the nodes shown, driven ones included.
- **Colors**: feedback couplings are now violet (the MDA color), so that they are not mistaken for the orange links of an optimizer.
- **Examples**:
  - laid out again: the tile on the left of its nodes, components in columns of two, sub-drivers stacked with room for their loops;
  - new `wing_sizing`: Aero, Structure and Performance coupled (weight ↔ maneuver load, solved by the MDA). The optimizer maximizes the range under a wing-loading constraint and finds 2,590 km (span 23.7 m, area 30.1 m², 7 evaluations).
  - `tools/smoke_installed.py` checks the new example: 10/10 examples pass.
- Found while building the example: `S` is a reserved name of sympy (as are `E`, `I`, `N`, `O`, `Q`). An analytic expression using it passes the static validation and fails in GEMSEO. A validation rule for these names is left for later.
