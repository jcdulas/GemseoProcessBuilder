# 83 — The 3D view from the scatter matrix

Status: Done
Depends on: 21, 80
SPEC: § 12.2

## Goal

Plan 80 opens an evaluation in `nastran_view` from the context menu of a row of the Data table and of a point of the XY plot, and from the best point of the Summary; its notes leave the scatter matrix out ("its points have no context menu yet"), although SPEC § 12.2 and SIZING_LOOP_EVOLUTIONS § 5 (GPB-E4) name it. The owner asked for it on 2026-10-23.

## Scope

In:
- `static/js/views/results/scatter_matrix.js`: the context menu of a point of every cell off the diagonal, **Open evaluation N in the 3D view** and **Compare in the 3D view** with the one other evaluation brushed (`showView3dMenu` of plan 80, its state in `lib/view3d.js`), and the tooltip of a point (its evaluation and its two values), as in the XY plot;
- the brush of a cell under its points, so that they receive the pointer (as in the XY plot);
- the user guide and the changelog.

Out:
- the binned cells (from `BINNING_THRESHOLD` evaluations): a bin is not an evaluation, no menu;
- the diagonal (histograms).

## Steps

- [x] The context menu and the tooltip of the points; the brush under them.
- [x] User guide, changelog, this plan.

## Tests

- The menu's state is `lib/view3d.js`'s, tested by `tests/js/view3d.test.js` (plan 80); the view itself has no DOM test, as the XY plot.
- `python tools/check.py` passes (JS syntax of the view, node tests).

## Implementation notes

- The points are drawn above the brush, so a brush is started from the empty part of a cell or from a point's surroundings, not on a point: the choice of the XY plot (plan 80), kept for the same reason (the tooltip and the menu of a point). Brushing still selects every point of the rectangle, including those it starts on.
- Not checked by hand from the window of the application (it needs `gemseo_nastran` and `nastran_view` in one Python of the runs, as plan 80's whole path).
