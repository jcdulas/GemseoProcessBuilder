# 38 — Drivers back to containers

Status: Done
Depends on: 37
SPEC: § 6.2, § 8.2

## Goal

The user asked: "the optimizer must behave like an assembly: the components under it must be invisible at its level". Plan 35 had drawn the nodes a driver drives next to it, at its level; this plan goes back to containers, and keeps what still helps.

## Scope

In:
- a driver is a container, like an assembly:
  - a card at its level;
  - double-click to enter it, or expand it in place;
  - double-click its name to open its editor;
- kept from plan 35:
  - the card with the icon of the kind;
  - dragging a node onto a driver (or from it) to put the node under its control;
  - a driver as a step of a chain (plan 36);
- removed:
  - the nodes of a driver drawn at its level;
  - the loop of driver links and its panel;
  - "Open alone".

Out:
- The start and end of a driver's level (plan 37) already show what it takes and gives back.

## Steps

- [x] Scene: drivers placed like assemblies; driver links removed (`lib/driver_links.js` deleted).
- [x] Canvas, menus, inspector, drawing and styles back to containers.
- [x] Tests, SPEC, guide, README screenshot.

## Tests

- `node --test`: a driver is a container (its nodes are not at its level, and shown when expanded in place); the start and end link to its card.

## Acceptance criteria

- At the root of the Sellar example: Start → Optimizer → End; the disciplines are inside the optimizer.
- `python tools/check.py` passes.

## Implementation notes

- `buildScene` no longer has tiles:
  - `isTile`, `TILE_GAP`, the "move aside" of tiles, `tileLinks` and `lib/driver_links.js` are removed;
  - the `driver` and `control` link kinds are removed, with their panel (`openDriverLinkPanel`), "Stop driving" and their inspector section.
- `views/canvas/drive.js` keeps `driveNode`: the link gesture between a driver and a node reparents the node into the driver.
- The start and end circles link a variable of a component inside a collapsed container to the card of the container.
- The examples keep their layout: the children of a driver were laid out in its level, which is where they are shown again.
- `tests/js/driver_tiles.test.js` became `workflow_scene.test.js`: the chain, parallel, start/end and container tests.
