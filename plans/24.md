# 24 — N2 view

Status: Done
Depends on: 11
SPEC: § 8.4, § 14.1 (N2 performance)

## Goal

Add the N2 matrix view of a scope, built from the resolver, rendered in SVG with virtualization so it stays fluid on very large models.

## Scope

In:
- Bridge method `n2.build(scope)`: ordered diagonal entries (components and collapsible containers), off-diagonal cells with coupled variable lists, hierarchy blocks. Computed in the UI process from the resolver (no GEMSEO).
- N2 center tab with its own zoom/pan, following the current canvas level by default (toggle "Follow canvas").
- Hierarchical blocks: collapse/expand an assembly or driver on the diagonal; collapsed entries aggregate their couplings.
- Cell interaction: hover highlights row/column; click shows the coupled variables in the inspector (and selects the producer and consumer on the canvas).
- Reordering by drag and drop on the diagonal: in `chain` assemblies it changes the execution order (a command); elsewhere it is a display order stored in the layout (non-undoable).
- Feedback cells (below the diagonal) in the feedback color.
- Virtualized rendering: only visible cells are in the DOM; below a zoom threshold, cells become plain colored squares without labels (level of detail).
- "Show in N2" context-menu action from canvas and tree.

Out:
- Performance tuning against the 2,000-component target (plan 32), image export (plan 31).

## Implementation notes

- Python (`core/n2.py`, bridge `n2.build` in `app/api_n2.py`):
  - The fully expanded matrix of a level:
    - `entries`: the leaves, meaning components, and drivers seen through their ports (a driver is a scope of its own; double-click it to open it);
    - `blocks`: the assemblies, each with the range of leaves it holds;
    - `cells`: variables, feedback flag, and `links` with the local ports, as in the canvas links.
  - Order: the model order, or the display order in `layout.extra["n2_order"]`. `chain` assemblies keep their execution order.
- Page, pure libraries:
  - `lib/n2_layout.js`:
    - `collapse` merges the rows and columns of collapsed blocks and drops their inner cells (aggregation happens here, so collapsing needs no bridge call);
    - `cellsByRow`;
    - `siblings` and `reorder` (a drop outside the container changes nothing).
  - `lib/viewport_cells.js`: `visibleRange` and `visibleCells` (partial cells at the edges included), and `cellsInView` for sparse rows.
- `views/n2/n2_view.js`, an "N2" center tab (View › N2 matrix, "Show in N2" in the canvas and tree menus):
  - Follows the canvas level by default; "Show in N2" shows the level holding the node, scrolls to it and highlights its label.
  - d3 zoom and pan. Cells are drawn as two paths (forward and feedback), limited to the visible rows and columns, with the visible diagonal rects and sticky row labels in a header column.
  - Level of detail: under 14 px per cell, no texts; under 9 px, no labels.
  - Hover: row and column bands, and a tooltip with the variables.
  - Click: the inspector lists the variables through `linkFocus`, with "Make explicit" between components. Double-click selects the producer and the consumer; on a driver, it opens its level.
  - Blocks: brackets in the header column collapse a block; a collapsed entry expands from its label or its "+" cell. Collapse all and Expand all refit the view.
  - Dragging a diagonal entry reorders it among its siblings: a `reparentNodes` command (undoable) in a `chain` assembly, otherwise `n2_order` in the layout (not undoable).
  - Elements are keyed by node id: indices change when blocks collapse.
- Smoke-tested:
  - Sellar: y_1 above the diagonal, y_2 as a feedback, and clicking the feedback cell lists y_2.
  - A generated 500-component model (10 groups, 1,490 couplings): 33 ms per zoom or pan step, measured over two animation frames, so rendering fits in a frame. It is smooth by hand too. Collapse all gives a 10 × 10 matrix of groups.
  - Drag reordering is stored in the layout.
